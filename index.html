Adaptive AP practice

Practice that brings back what you miss.Practice AP. Review what you miss.

AP-style questions from a built-in bank for 13 AP courses. The ones you miss come back on a spaced schedule until they stick.

    New here? The whole loop is 4 steps.

    1. Generate AP-style questions for a course, topic, and difficulty from the built-in bank. With an AI connection, from your own notes too.
    2. Practice the set, one question at a time.
    3. See your score, XP, and topic growth the moment you finish.
    4. Review your mistakes. They come back on a spaced schedule until they stick.

    No API key needed. The demo works out of the box.

      Your data

      Your mistake log, topic mastery, XP, streak, and settings are stored in this browser. Back them up, or restore them on another device. Restoring replaces those; before it does, it shows the backup's date and how many mistakes it holds.

      Not in a backup: the Notes box, the practice set in progress, and answers still waiting to upload. They stay in this browser.
