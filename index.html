Start studying

New here? The whole loop is 4 steps.

  1. Generate AP-style questions from a subject, topic, and difficulty, or from your own notes.
  2. Practice the set, one question at a time.
  3. See your score, XP, and topic growth the moment you finish.
  4. Review your mistakes. They come back on a spaced schedule until they stick.

No API key needed. The demo works out of the box.

    Setup & connection

    Connect an AI model and back up your data. Practice works without any of this.

    Connection

    API key: edit the project file .env (fill in OPENAI_API_KEY), save, then restart server.py. The key stays on the server, never in this page. Open via http://127.0.0.1:8765/, not file://.

    Your data

    Your mistake log and topic mastery are stored in this browser. Back them up, or restore them on another device.
