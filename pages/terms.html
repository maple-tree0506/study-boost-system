What this is

StudyBoost is a free study tool for AP students. There is no account, no subscription, and no payment. You may use it for your own studying, and teachers may use it with their classes.

Accuracy — please read this one

Practice questions come from a built-in question bank or, when an API key is configured, from an AI model. Both can be wrong. AI-generated questions and explanations may contain factual errors, and no question here is an official College Board item.

Treat StudyBoost as practice, not as an authority. For anything that matters, check your textbook, your teacher, or official College Board materials. Do not rely on it as your only preparation for an exam.

Your data is your responsibility

Your progress is stored in your browser, not on our servers. Clearing your browser data, switching devices, or using private browsing will lose it. Use Download my data if you want a backup. We cannot recover progress that your browser has discarded.
