About StudyBoost

Most study tools give you a pile of flashcards and leave the hard part to you: deciding what to review next. StudyBoost closes that loop.

How it works

  1. Generate — pick an AP course, topic, and difficulty, or paste your own class notes.
  2. Practice — work through the set one question at a time.
  3. See the result — score, XP, level, and how your mastery of that topic moved.
  4. Review — every question you miss enters a spaced-repetition schedule (the SM-2 algorithm) and comes back at widening intervals until it sticks.

Why spaced repetition

Rereading notes feels productive because the material looks familiar, but recognition is not recall. Retrieving an answer from memory, at increasing intervals, is what builds durable knowledge. StudyBoost schedules that for you instead of asking you to plan it.

What it is honest about

  • Questions are AP-style, written independently — never copied College Board items.
  • The offline demo uses a fixed bank, so it will repeat once you have worked through a difficulty tier. Adding an API key unlocks unlimited AI-generated questions.
  • AI can be wrong. See the Terms.

Who built it

StudyBoost is an independent project built by a high-school student who wanted a study tool that actually decided what to review next. It is free, collects no personal information, and is not affiliated with the College Board.
