Privacy

Last updated: 2026-09-01

StudyBoost has no accounts. You are never asked for a name, an email address, or a password, and there is nothing to sign up for.

What stays on your device

Almost everything. The following is kept in your browser's local storage and is never uploaded:

  • Your mistake log and its spaced-repetition schedule
  • Your topic-mastery estimates
  • Your XP, level, streak, and daily totals
  • Interface preferences and whether you have seen the welcome card
  • Any notes you paste into the notes box

Because this lives in your browser, clearing your browser data erases it, and it does not follow you to another device. You can back it up yourself with Download my data under Setup & connection.

What is sent to our server

One row per graded question, containing exactly six fields:

  • the AP subject
  • the question type (multiple choice or short answer)
  • whether the answer was correct
  • a timestamp
  • a random anonymous ID generated in your browser (for example u-3f9c…)
  • whether the question was newly generated or resurfaced for review

The anonymous ID is a random value with no connection to your identity. It exists so your own Progress panel can show your own results. The text of your notes, your answers, and the questions themselves are never sent to or stored on our server. This data is used only to understand whether the study loop works.

Third parties

  • jsDelivr serves the KaTeX stylesheet used to display formulas. Loading it reveals your IP address and browser to that CDN, as with any embedded web resource.
  • Google Forms is used only if you choose to open the feedback form. Nothing is sent unless you submit it.
  • An AI provider receives your notes and topic only if an API key has been configured on the server. The public demo runs without a key, so it generates questions from a built-in offline bank and contacts no AI provider.

Students under 13

StudyBoost is built for high-school AP students. It collects no personal information from anyone, of any age. If you are under 13, please use it with a parent's or teacher's knowledge.

Deleting your data

Clearing your browser's site data for this site removes everything stored locally. To have the anonymous practice rows removed from the server as well, ask through the feedback form and include nothing more than the anonymous ID shown in your browser's storage.
